A model of the cognitive, affective, and behavioral stages people experience when doing research.

How to use this page

Press Play to follow Maya, a student writing a research paper, through the six stages. Each step is shown for a few seconds before the next one appears. Press Pause at any time.

To go at your own pace, use Previous and Next, or choose a stage by its number. Keyboard users can press Tab to reach the stage list and the left and right arrow keys to move between stages.
